Speedtest

The speedtest measures the maximum download speed for you computer and internet connection.

How do I read these results?

Current Speed is your average download speed in the past 5 seconds.

Average Speed is your average download speed during the entire test.

If your speed is slower than what you are paying for, your provider is limiting your connection, or your router is the bottleneck.

If you want faster download speeds, try FDM (Free Download Manager).
