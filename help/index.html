Help

How do I upload a file?

Click "Choose Files" on the main page, select one or more files, then click "Upload". Once the upload is complete, you'll receive a download link.

Is there a file size limit?

No. There are no file size limits.

How long are files stored?

I don't know, I am still testing that. Free Upload Manager is not made for permanent private storage, it's a service for sending HUGE files over the internet.

Where do my uploaded files go?

When you upload a file on FUM, they are being uploaded and stored on Buzzheavier via their API. We use Oracle Cloud as a proxy to upload the files to Buzzheavier.

How do I view my upload history?

Create an account or log in, then visit My Account to see all files you've uploaded.

What is the account ID?

Your account ID is a unique code that identifies your uploads. No password or email required, just keep it secret. You can create one for free on the Login / Signup page.

How do I share my link?

You copy the link and then paste it.
