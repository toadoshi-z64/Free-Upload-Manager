Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

What we collect

Free Upload Manager does not collect, store, or log any personal information. We do not store your IP address, email address, or any other identifying information when you use the service.

Uploaded files

Files you upload are stored on third-party hosting providers (BuzzHeavier and FileDitch) depending on your selection. We do not have access to your uploaded files. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for details on how they handle your data.

Cookies & local storage

We use browser local storage solely to remember your account session (if you choose to create an account). No tracking cookies or third-party analytics are used.

Third-party services

BuzzHeavier and FileDitch are independent services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We are not responsible for how they handle data.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or need support, visit our Contact page.
